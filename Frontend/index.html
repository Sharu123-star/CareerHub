ABOUT CAREERHUB

Build Your Career with CareerHub

CareerHub is a student career management platform designed to help students organize their professional information and discover internship opportunities in one place.

Students can create their profile, add technical skills, showcase projects and certifications, explore internships, and track their applications easily.

CareerHub also provides an admin platform to manage internship opportunities and update application statuses, making the recruitment process easier to manage.
